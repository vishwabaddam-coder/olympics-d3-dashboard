06 — Participation × Medals

Does Delegation Size Matter?

Each circle is a country. X-axis: athletes sent. Y-axis: total medals won. Circle radius also encodes total medals. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan.

Delegation Size vs Medal Haul

Colour indicates continent

This plot shows an association, not causation. Larger delegations can enter more events, which mechanically increases medal opportunity — but many other factors (funding, training, talent depth) also matter. No causal claim is made.
